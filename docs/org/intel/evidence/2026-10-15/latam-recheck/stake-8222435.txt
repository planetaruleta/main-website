Local Currency: Can I use my credit card for deposit?
August 24, 2026

We now offer the option to make deposits in selected local currencies using your Visa or Mastercard. Credit card deposits are only available for supported currencies, please check this article to learn more.

 

Q: How do I make a deposit via credit card?

A:

Go to the Wallet tab.

Select Deposit > Local Currency > Debit/Credit Card.

Select your desired deposit amount.

Enter your card details and click Set Amount to proceed.

 

 

Q: I can’t find the credit card option. What should I do?

A: Please check whether your preferred currency is supported in your country. The credit card option will only appear if it is available for your selected currency and location.

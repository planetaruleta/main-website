Can I use my credit card for withdrawal?

August 22, 2026

We now offer the option to withdraw in selected local currencies back to your Visa or Mastercard. Credit card withdrawals are only available for supported currencies, please check this article to learn more.

Q: Who can withdraw to a credit card?

A: Credit card withdrawals are available only to users who have previously made a deposit using a credit card.

Q: Can I withdraw to a different credit card?

A: No. Credit card withdrawals are processed back to the original card used for the deposit.

Q: How long does a credit card withdrawal take?

A: After the withdrawal is processed from the Stake account, it may take up to 3 business days for the funds to reach your bank account.
